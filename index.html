My top 3 movies of all-time.

Bohemian Rhapsody

Bohemian Rhapsody is an inspiring and emotional film that shows Freddie Mercury’s incredible talent and complex personality. The powerful music and live performance scenes make it unforgettable and deeply moving.

Harry Potter

Harry Potter is a magical and captivating story about friendship, courage, and growing up. The rich world of Hogwarts and the characters’ journeys make the series timeless and unforgettable.

Interstellar

Interstellar is a visually stunning and emotionally powerful film that explores love, time, and humanity’s survival. Its deep scientific themes combined with strong human drama make it unforgettable.
